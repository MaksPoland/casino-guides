Recognizing Signs of Problem Gambling

It’s important to be aware of the signs that may indicate you or someone else is experiencing issues with gambling. Here are some common indicators:

If you recognize any of these signs in yourself or someone you know, it may be time to seek help. Numerous resources are available, including support groups and helplines, where you can receive guidance and support.
