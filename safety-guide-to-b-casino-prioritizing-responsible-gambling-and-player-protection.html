Recognizing Signs of Problem Gambling

It’s essential to be aware of the signs that may indicate problematic gambling behavior. If you or someone you know exhibits any of the following signs, it may be time to seek help:

  • Feeling the need to gamble with increasing amounts of money to achieve the desired excitement.
  • Repeated unsuccessful attempts to control, cut back, or stop gambling.
  • Gambling to escape from problems or to relieve feelings of helplessness, guilt, or anxiety.
  • Jeopardizing or losing a significant relationship, job, or education or career opportunity because of gambling.

By recognizing these signs early, you can take proactive steps to maintain a healthy relationship with gambling.
